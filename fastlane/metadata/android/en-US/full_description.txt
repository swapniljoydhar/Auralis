Tonalore is a private, local-first audio player for Android, derived from Auxio and designed for a focused native listening experience.

<b>Features</b>

- Playback based on Media3 ExoPlayer
- Snappy UI derived from the latest Material Design guidelines
- Opinionated UX that prioritizes ease of use over edge cases
- Customizable behavior
- Support for disc numbers, multiple artists, release types, precise/original dates, sort tags, and more
- Advanced artist system that unifies artists and album artists
- SD Card-aware folder management
- Reliable playlisting functionality
- Playback state persistence
- Android Auto support
- Automatic gapless playback
- Full ReplayGain support on MP3, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, and MP4 files
- External equalizer support
- Edge-to-edge
- Embedded covers support
- Search functionality
- Headset autoplay
- Stylish widgets that automatically adapt to their size
- Completely private and offline

This fork keeps the existing Music experience intact while the native Audiobooks experience is developed as a separate product mode for supported local audio formats.

Tonalore remains subject to the original Auxio project’s GPLv3 license and attribution requirements.
