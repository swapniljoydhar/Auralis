Auralis brings music and audiobooks together in one focused, private application. The opening hub lets you choose Music or Audiobooks, and each mode exposes the controls that fit that medium.

<b>Music</b>

- Playback based on Media3 ExoPlayer
- Snappy UI derived from the latest Material Design guidelines
- Opinionated UX that prioritizes ease of use over edge cases
- Customizable behavior
- Support for disc numbers, multiple artists, release types, precise/original dates, sort tags, and more
- Advanced artist system that unifies artists and album artists
- SD Card-aware folder management
- Reliable playlisting functionality
- Playback state persistence
- Android Auto support
- Automatic gapless playback
- Full ReplayGain support (On MP3, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, and MP4 files)
- External equalizer support (ex. Wavelet)
- Edge-to-edge
- Embedded covers support
- Search functionality
- Headset autoplay
- Stylish widgets that automatically adapt to their size

<b>Audiobooks</b>

- M4B book and MP3 chapter folder support
- Chapter navigation with duration and completion state
- Resume from last position
- Bookmarks with jump-to-bookmark
- Configurable skip intervals
- Playback speed control
- Auto-rewind on resume
- Silence skipping
- Sleep timer (15/30/60 minutes or end of chapter)

<b>Privacy</b>

- Completely private and offline
- No account required
- No cloud synchronization
- Library metadata, playback state, bookmarks, and settings stay on your device
